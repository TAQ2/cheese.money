⇢ The 2D grid

Left→right is your flow with WIP protection. Top→bottom are swim lanes: your lane on top, one autonomous lane per agent below. Ownership is structural — Working cells exist only in agent lanes; Desk and Proving exist only in yours.

Parts 21, 25 — capacity allocation via swim lanes with explicit limits

↓↑ Dive & re-emerge

Committing work sends it down into an agent lane; the lane runs autonomously; the card rises back to your lane the moment it needs you — finished, or blocked on something only you can resolve. Needs-me is the buffer that keeps the bottleneck (you) fed but never flooded.

Parts 20, 32 — buffer in front of the bottleneck; issue escalation

✨ Auto-filed, 📌 pinned

The same background task that regenerates conversation titles doubles as the status classifier: it files each conversation into a cell (✨). A manual drag always wins and pins the card (📌) so the classifier won’t second-guess you.
